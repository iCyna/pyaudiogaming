========================================================================
MODULE: pyaudiogaming.sound_pool
sound_pool — BASS Audio Engine, 3D HRTF, Steam Audio & Recording
========================================================================

SUMMARY:
Full-featured BASS audio engine: output/input device management, streaming & cached samples, fading, binaural HRTF, Steam Audio wall occlusion, push streams, and microphone recording.
