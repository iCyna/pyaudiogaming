========================================================================
MODULE: pyaudiogaming.vb
vb — Virtual Box (Accessible Input, Rich Forms & Modal Boxes)
========================================================================

SUMMARY:
NVDA & TalkBack accessible UI engine featuring advanced text input (with Ctrl+Shift+Alt+Backspace neutral keyboard mode switcher), 23-control forms (group, tab, link, switch, table, tree, slider), and ready-to-use modal boxes.
