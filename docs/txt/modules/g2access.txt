========================================================================
MODULE: pyaudiogaming.g2access
g2access — Compact High-Performance Screen Reader & BASS-Routed TTS Engine
========================================================================

SUMMARY:
Zero-bloat accessibility & TTS router with shared AudioPipeline (BASS PCM, LRU cache, 3D/HRTF/FX) and dedicated OS drivers: windows.py, android.py, ios.py, macos.py, linux.py.

[MODULE FUNCTIONS]
----------------------------------------
* create_providers() -> any
* load_native_lib(name32, name64=None) -> any

* pcm_to_wav(pcm_bytes, sample_rate=22050, channels=1, bits=16) -> any
  Wrap raw PCM bytes with a standard 44-byte RIFF/WAVE header.
