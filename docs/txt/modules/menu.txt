========================================================================
MODULE: pyaudiogaming.menu
menu — Non-Blocking Accessible Menus
========================================================================

SUMMARY:
Accessible game menu supporting keyboard arrows, '&K' shortcut hotkeys, wrap-around navigation, custom cursor/enter/cancel sounds, and mobile TalkBack touch gestures.


[CLASS menu]
----------------------------------------
