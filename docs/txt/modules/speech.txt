MODULE: pyaudiogaming.speech
speech — Screen Reader, TTS & 3D Positional Speech
========================================================================

SUMMARY:
Unified speech wrapper supporting NVDA, JAWS, SAPI5, VoiceOver, TalkBack, speech history, voice/rate/pitch/volume controls, and 3D positional TTS.
